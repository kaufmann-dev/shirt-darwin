# Shirt Darwin

Shirt Darwin is an experiment-driven print-on-demand business. It uses AI and human judgment to discover niche communities, create relevant T-shirt designs, test them with paid traffic, and invest in the ideas that produce real profit.

## The model

One shared commerce platform powers many niche storefronts. Each niche gets its own branded subdomain and designs:

```text
birdwatching.example.com
climbers.example.com
woodworkers.example.com
```

The storefronts share the same underlying product, fulfillment, analytics, and advertising systems. Printify or a similar provider produces and ships each order.

## The loop

1. Research niche communities and identify promising interests.
2. Generate designs and a focused storefront for each niche.
3. Run small, controlled Meta ad tests.
4. Measure sales, costs, and contribution profit.
5. Improve promising niches and stop weak experiments.
6. Reinvest in repeatable winners.
7. Launch a new batch each week.

## Decision metric

Experiments are judged by contribution profit, not revenue alone:

```text
revenue
- production and shipping
- payment and platform fees
- taxes
- refunds and replacements
- advertising spend
= contribution profit
```

## Principles

- AI proposes, builds, analyzes, and recommends; a human approves consequential decisions.
- Every experiment has a fixed budget and a clear stopping rule.
- Niches earn more investment through evidence, not intuition.
- Designs must respect trademarks, copyright, and community norms.
- Customer experience and product quality remain consistent across storefronts.
- Failed tests produce reusable knowledge rather than silent deletion.

## Initial goal

Build the smallest system that can launch one niche storefront, fulfill one product through a print-on-demand provider, attribute one ad-driven purchase, and calculate its true contribution profit. Automate and scale only after that loop works end to end.
